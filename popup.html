Quick delete

Hover over a chat in the sidebar and click the red 🗑 button. It's deleted right away.

Bulk delete

  1. Click Bulk delete in the bar at the top of the page, or under On this page here.
  2. Click chats to select (Shift-click for a range).
  3. For everything: Load all, then Select all.
  4. Click Delete, then click again to confirm.
  • Stop halts a run. Esc leaves selection mode.
  • Keep the tab open while chats are deleted.

Deleted chats can't be restored.

If it stops working

Google sometimes changes these pages. Chats that couldn't be deleted get a red outline. To capture what went wrong:

  1. Click 🐞 in the bar (turn the floating bar on for this). It turns red and starts recording.
  2. Delete one chat by hand: ⋮ → Delete → confirm.
  3. Click 🐞 again to copy the debug info, then share it with whoever maintains the extension.

Chat titles and links are masked in the debug info.
